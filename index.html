TUKO Personal Assistant

A private assistant that reads its owner's own email and calendar, with the owner's permission, and prepares a short morning briefing: what changed, what needs attention and what to do today.

Daily briefing

One email each morning to its owner, summarising the day ahead.

Calendar awareness

Upcoming events, schedule conflicts and preparation for trips and appointments.

Email follow-ups

Messages that need a reply and conversations still waiting for an answer.

Read-only access

TUKO reads Gmail and Google Calendar. It does not send, delete or change them.

TUKO is a personal, single-user project. It is not offered as a public service and does not accept new users. Google account access is used only for the owner's own account.
